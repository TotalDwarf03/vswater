# vswater Roadmap 💧

This document outlines the future vision and planned features for the vswater extension. Features are categorized by their primary goal.

## 🏆 Gamification & Motivation
*Goal: Make hydration fun and habit-forming.*

- [ ] **Hydration Streaks:** Track and display the number of consecutive days the daily goal is reached.
- [ ] **Achievements & Badges:** Unlock visual badges for milestones (e.g., "7-Day Warrior", "10L Total").
- [ ] **XP & Leveling System:** Earn experience points for every ml logged, leveling up your "Hydration Rank."

## 🧠 Smart Features
*Goal: Make the extension adapt to the user's workflow.*

- [ ] **Working Hours:** Configure specific times and days for reminders (e.g., Mon-Fri, 9 AM - 5 PM).
- [ ] **Intelligent Intervals:** Automatically shorten reminder intervals if the user is behind their daily goal.
- [ ] **Focus Mode Integration:** Automatically silence reminders during active debugging sessions or when in "Zen Mode."

## ✨ Visual Polish & UX
*Goal: Improve the look and feel of the dashboard and notifications.*

- [ ] **Animated Water:** Add "wave" animations and rising bubbles to the SVG glass in the sidebar.
- [ ] **Themeable Containers:** Choose between different visuals: a standard glass, a sports bottle, or a coffee mug.
- [ ] **Interactive History:** Click on historical days in the sidebar to see a breakdown of logs for that day.
- [ ] **Unit Support (oz):** Toggle between milliliters (ml) and fluid ounces (oz).

## 📊 Advanced Analytics
*Goal: Provide deeper insights into hydration habits.*

- [ ] **Monthly Heat Map:** A calendar view showing hydration consistency over the month.
- [ ] **Data Export:** Export hydration history to CSV or JSON for external use.
- [ ] **Trends Analysis:** Show "best hydration time of day" or "average daily intake" over time.

## 🌐 Accessibility & Localization
*Goal: Make vswater available to everyone.*

- [ ] **Internationalization (i18n):** Support for multiple languages.
- [ ] **Screen Reader Support:** Ensure all Webview elements are fully accessible.

---

*This roadmap is a living document and will be updated as new ideas emerge and features are implemented.*
